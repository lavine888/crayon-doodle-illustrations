# 生图提示词模板

每页单独生成，不要把多页拼成一张。

```text
Generate one standalone vertical Chinese editorial knowledge-card illustration in a handmade colored-pencil / crayon doodle style.

Canvas:
3:4 vertical composition by default. Warm ivory / cream paper background with subtle natural paper grain. No platform UI, no carousel dots, no page-count pill, no navigation arrows.

Visual DNA:
Hand-drawn Chinese typography, visible colored-pencil and crayon texture, imperfect slightly wobbly linework, soft graphite shading, vermilion-orange + cobalt-blue + graphite-gray palette. Feels like a smart creator's notebook or handmade explainer page, not a polished vector infographic.

Theme:
{本页主题}

Core judgment:
{这页唯一要让读者记住的一句话}

Title text:
{1-3行中文标题}

Body text:
{2-6行短解释}

Optional emphasized line:
{0-2行橙红强调句}

Visual metaphor:
{这页的具体物理隐喻}

Composition:
Top 20%-28%: large handwritten title, mostly vermilion-orange and cobalt-blue.
Middle 18%-25%: short handwritten explanatory lines in cobalt-blue, with at most one or two orange emphasized lines.
Bottom 45%-58%: one coherent hand-drawn metaphor scene. Let the illustration carry the idea instead of merely decorating the text.

Characters:
Use generic young creators / users / engineers / customers only if helpful. Natural proportions, simple facial features, clear gestures. Blue clothing and a small orange accessory may be used as a recurring visual anchor, but do not create or copy an exact fixed IP character.

Texture:
Visible dry colored-pencil strokes, crayon edges, light crosshatching, uneven pigment density, slight hand-drawn imperfections. Avoid smooth digital gradients and perfect vector contours.

Constraints:
One page = one cognitive point.
Do not copy any specific reference-image composition, character, machine, bridge, chicken, helicopter, river, or wording.
Do not add decorative icons just to fill space.
Do not make it look like a PPT slide, corporate infographic, UI screen, 3D render, Pixar/cartoon poster, children's picture book, or cyberpunk artwork.
Do not add app UI elements such as 1/9, arrow buttons, bottom dots, frames, or navigation controls.
Keep Chinese text legible, concise, and visually integrated with the drawing.
```

## 封面模板

```text
Generate one standalone 3:4 vertical cover in the same handmade colored-pencil / crayon doodle visual system.
Use a warm cream paper background, large handwritten Chinese title in vermilion-orange and cobalt-blue, and one strong metaphor illustration occupying the lower half.
Keep the cover simpler than an interior page: no dense body copy, no UI, no carousel indicators.
The image should feel editorial, clever, handmade, and immediately readable at small mobile size.
```

## 修图：去平台 UI

```text
Edit the provided image. Remove only the platform UI overlays: page-count pill, left/right navigation arrows, bottom carousel dots, app border, or other screenshot controls. Reconstruct the missing area with the same warm cream paper and matching hand-drawn background. Preserve the illustration, typography, texture, composition, and colors.
```

## 修图：增强彩铅质感

```text
Regenerate the same concept and layout with more authentic colored-pencil / crayon media texture: visible dry pigment, uneven hand pressure, pencil crosshatching, slightly rough edges, subtle paper grain, and imperfect hand-drawn Chinese lettering. Keep the palette restrained to vermilion-orange, cobalt-blue, and graphite gray. Avoid vector smoothness.
```
