# Benchmark Rubric

Use this rubric to decide whether a generated card actually belongs to this visual system.

Score each dimension 0-2. A publishable image should normally score at least 16/20, with no zero in the first five dimensions.

## 1. Paper

- 0: pure digital white, dark background, gradient, or obvious UI surface.
- 1: warm paper exists but feels flat or synthetic.
- 2: stable warm ivory paper with subtle handmade feel and clean negative space.

## 2. Material

- 0: vector, glossy, 3D, photorealistic, or smooth digital paint.
- 1: some hand-drawn lines but texture is weak.
- 2: visible dry colored-pencil / crayon grain, uneven pressure, imperfect fills.

## 3. Palette

- 0: many unrelated colors or neon tech palette.
- 1: orange/blue appear but without hierarchy.
- 2: vermilion-orange for emphasis, cobalt-blue for explanation, graphite for structure.

## 4. Typography

- 0: typeset UI font or dense text wall.
- 1: hand-written feeling exists but hierarchy is weak.
- 2: clear handmade Chinese headline, short explanatory lines, controlled color emphasis.

## 5. Metaphor

- 0: decorative illustration unrelated to the argument.
- 1: concept is represented literally but works.
- 2: one fresh physical metaphor makes the abstract relationship memorable.

## 6. Composition

- 0: PPT grid, card dashboard, or clutter.
- 1: readable but too balanced / mechanical.
- 2: strong top-text / bottom-scene hierarchy, natural asymmetry, generous breathing room.

## 7. Character use

- 0: mascot posing or copied reference character.
- 1: character is relevant but passive.
- 2: any character performs the conceptual action and feels like part of the scene.

If no character is needed, score 2 when the scene communicates clearly without one.

## 8. Series consistency

- 0: visually unrelated to adjacent pages.
- 1: palette matches but layout or texture drifts.
- 2: same visual world, while composition still changes page to page.

## 9. Text accuracy

- 0: major Chinese errors or unreadable headline.
- 1: tiny defects in secondary labels.
- 2: headline and key labels are correct and readable.

## 10. Originality

- 0: directly copies a reference composition, distinctive character, wording, or prop arrangement.
- 1: generic stock metaphor.
- 2: fresh scene built from the current topic while preserving only the visual grammar.

## Hard failures

Regardless of total score, reject when:

- platform UI is visible (`1/9`, arrows, dots, app controls)
- a third-party watermark or logo appears
- text changes the intended meaning
- the page becomes a formal PPT infographic
- the image directly reproduces a reference screenshot's distinctive composition
